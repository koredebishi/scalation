\frametitle{Digital Taxation Dilemma: The Shankar Case}

\textbf{Facts:} Vegetable vendor Shankar processed Rs 16.3M (\$178k) digitally.
Received Rs 2.9M (\$32k) GST demand. Groceries are tax-exempt, but transactions lacked formal invoicing.

\vspace{0.2cm}
\textbf{Development:} Government is flagging more sellers while warning against shifting to cash.

\vspace{0.3cm}

\begin{columns}[T]
\begin{column}{0.48\textwidth}
\textbf{Pro-Tax:} Large volumes deserve scrutiny.\\[0.15cm]
\textbf{Government:} Don't hide earnings by going cash.
\end{column}
\begin{column}{0.48\textwidth}
\textbf{Anti-Tax:} Will push vendors to cash, hurt digitization.\\[0.15cm]
\textbf{Market:} Small sellers threaten strike.
\end{column}
\end{columns}

\vspace{0.3cm}

\textbf{Discussion Questions:}
\begin{itemize}\itemsep0em
\item Identify an ethical issue; apply a workable theory
\item Did sellers do anything wrong? Who benefited? Who was hurt?
\item Did sellers have unfair advantages? Are they deceitful?
\item Propose a fair solution
\end{itemize}
