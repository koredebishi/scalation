# Eaton Fire Corridor — Research Objectives

---

## What We Are Building

1. **Delta Analysis**
   Fire day vs. baseline: flow surge magnitude, speed collapse onset time, and spatial propagation along the I-210 / SR-134 corridor.

2. **Demand Profile**
   Simulation-ready entry/exit flows and junction split ratios — including gap-filled ramp data for sensors with missing readings.

3. **Contraflow Control Logic**
   Time-triggered lane reversal rules designed to maximize outbound capacity on I-210 WB and SR-134 WB during a wildfire evacuation.

4. **Microsimulation Validation**
   Scalation replay of January 7 (uncontrolled evacuation) vs. the contraflow scenario.
   **Success metric: reduced corridor clearance time.**
