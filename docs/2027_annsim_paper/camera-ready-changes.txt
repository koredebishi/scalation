REVIEWER #2
-------------------------------------------------------------
Comment 1: "There should be a presentation of the architecture
of the simulation: how do numerical integration, discrete events
simulation and arrival processes interplay?"

---------------------------------------------------------
Added a new system architecture diagram (Figure 3) in Section
4.3. which shows the three external configurations feeding into the
TrafficModelBuilder, which assembles the engine. Inside the
engine, two panels sit side by side: Arrival & Network
(per-lane VSource, Poisson/Erlang2S, Route, Junction sensors)
and Vehicle Physics (car-following models, eight integrators).

discrete-continuous time model.

Comment 3: "Add a short discussion on whether the
integrator-insensitivity result is expected to hold beyond
IDM-like continuous models."

---------------------------------------------------------
Added to the Discussion (Section 6). The integrator
insensitivity is tied to IDM's smooth ODE formulation. In preliminary study we ran
a comparison with Gipps, which is piecewise — it matched flow
(NRMSE 2.11% vs 2.12%) but degraded speed significantly
(17.23% vs 12.56%). So the result does not generalize to all
car-following models with respect to speed.

---------------------------------------------------------
That was our mistake — we entered a draft summary into START
instead of the actual abstract. The camera-ready PDF has the
correct final abstract.
